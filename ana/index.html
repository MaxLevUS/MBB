Ana is a bridal makeup artist and hairstylist in Minnesota, with experience creating looks for weddings, photoshoots, celebrations and other special occasions.

She focuses on enhancing natural features with polished makeup and thoughtfully styled hair. Every look is shaped around the bride's personal style, inspiration and wedding-day plans.

Ana is an artist with Minnesota Bridal Beauty. All inquiries and scheduling for MBB bookings are managed by our team.
